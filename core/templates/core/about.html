About SevaSetu

A simple platform for meaningful community action.

SevaSetu is an NGO Management System created to bring campaigns, volunteers, donations and community communication into one easy-to-use platform.

The project provides a practical digital space where people can discover social campaigns, contribute their time, support causes and stay connected with NGO activities. It also helps administrators keep important records organized in one place.

Purpose Connect people with causes.
Platform Django-based NGO management.
Focus Campaigns, volunteers and donations.
01

Discover

Explore campaigns and community initiatives in one place.

02

Participate

Volunteer your skills, time and support for selected campaigns.

03

Manage

Keep campaign, volunteer, donation and contact records organized.

Project vision

Make social participation easier to access, understand and manage.

SevaSetu is designed as a focused academic project, combining a clean user interface with simple database-driven features that can be extended as the platform grows.
